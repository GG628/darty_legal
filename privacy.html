Privacy Policy for Darty

Last Updated: 8 October 2026

Darty ("we", "our" or "us") is a darts scoring and statistics app, developed and operated in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what data Darty collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Summary (Plain English)

  • No account needed. Darty works fully offline. Your matches and stats live on your phone.
  • Accounts are optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, Darty keeps a private backup of your stats and lets you add friends, share matches, give kudos and comment.
  • Your backup is private. Only you can restore it. Friends never see it.
  • Friends see the matches you share — a summary of each match, not your whole database. You can keep any match private.
  • Delete your account in the app and everything on our servers goes with it. Your stats stay on your phone.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Whether or not you have an account, Darty stores these on your device:

  • Matches, scores and statistics
  • Tournament, training and career progress
  • Your profile (name, nationality, avatar), your dart sets and the players you play against
  • Checkout photos you choose to take
  • Settings and achievements

Without an account, none of this leaves your device. With an account, see Section 2 for what is copied to our servers.

Deletion: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Data Management to reset your stats.

2. If You Create an Account (Optional)

Signing in is optional. It turns on stats backups and the social features. If you sign in, we store the following on our servers, provided by Supabase in the EU (Ireland).

2.1 Sign-in

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive a unique account identifier and, from the provider, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and your name if you choose to share it. We use your email only to run your account. It is never shown to other players.

2.2 Your profile

Your username, display name, nationality and avatar (its settings, and a link to its image). Your profile is visible to other signed-in players, who can find you by searching for your username, unless one of you has blocked the other.

2.3 Your stats backup

A compressed copy of the stats database on your phone (matches, legs, dart sets, tournaments, career and settings), so a new phone can restore it. Checkout photos are not included. Only you can access your backup. We keep one copy, replaced each time you back up.

2.4 Shared matches (the feed)

For each match, a summary: when it was played, the game mode and format, the score, averages, darts thrown, best checkout, fastest leg, how your visits were scored, the venue, the name of your dart set, your opponent's name and the title of the match. For a real opponent (someone you entered as a local player) we upload their first name only. When you first sign in, your earlier matches are uploaded too, but your friends are not notified about them.

Shared matches are visible to you and your accepted friends. You can make any match private from its summary, so only you see it.

2.5 Friends, kudos and comments

Your friend requests and friends; the kudos and comments you give and receive; the players you block; and any reports you make about content or players. Kudos and comments are visible to anyone who can see the match they are on.

2.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device and your notification settings, and keep a record of the notifications we send you (for example, "Dave gave you kudos"). Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging, and may include the avatar image of the player they are about.

3. Optional Email for Updates

In Settings → Stay in the loop you can give us an email address so we can reply to your feedback or tell you about major updates. This is separate from your account. It is stored with our analytics provider, PostHog (EU), against your app installation, and you can remove it at any time from the same screen.

4. Analytics, Crash Reports and Updates

4.1 Usage analytics (PostHog)

  • What: app usage events (for example, app opened, match completed, training started), the screens you view, device model, operating system and app version, and answers to optional in-app surveys.
  • Linked to: a random identifier for your installation (and your email, if you gave one in Section 3). Analytics are not linked to your Darty account.
  • Not included: your scores, stats or player names.
  • Where: EU (eu.i.posthog.com). PostHog privacy policy

4.2 Crash reports (Sentry)

  • What: error details, device model, operating system and app version, and the sequence of app actions leading to a crash.
  • Not included: your name, email or game data.
  • Retention: 90 days. Sentry privacy policy

4.3 App updates and stability (Expo, EAS Insights)

  • What: an anonymous installation identifier, device model, operating system and app version, and session information, used to deliver over-the-air updates and monitor stability.
  • Expo privacy policy

7. Deleting Your Data

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account & backup → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, backup, shared matches, kudos, comments, friendships, blocks, devices and notifications from our servers. The stats on your phone are not affected.
  • Sign out: keeps your account and backup so you can sign back in.
  • Delete a match or a comment: deleting a match on your phone removes it from the feed at the next sync; you can delete your own comments, and any comment on your own match.
  • Everything else: email danrush730@gmail.com and we will delete analytics, crash or account data we hold about you.

8. Community Content

Comments, match titles, usernames and display names are visible to other players. We filter objectionable words, you can report comments and players from the app, and you can block anyone. We review reports and remove content or accounts that break our Terms of Service.

9. Children

Darty can be used by anyone without an account. You must be 13 or older to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has created one, contact us and we will delete it.

10. What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising.
  • We don't track your location, or access your contacts or other files. (Checkout photos are taken only when you choose and stay on your phone.)

11. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app (see Section 7). For anything else, email danrush730@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

We rely on these lawful bases: providing the service you ask for (your account, backups and social features); our legitimate interests in keeping the app working and improving it (crash reports and analytics); and your consent (optional email, and push notifications).

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Account data is held by Supabase, where access rules ensure players can read only what this policy allows, and data is encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last Updated" date will change, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app or in the update notes.
